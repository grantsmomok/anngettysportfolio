About This Condition

Some children are born with rare genetic conditions that affect how their lungs make or recycle surfactant — a substance the lungs need to stay open and work properly. SP‑C deficiency is one of these conditions.

In children with SP‑C deficiency, a change in the SFTPC gene causes a key lung protein to stop working correctly. Over time, this damages the lung cells and makes breathing harder.

This module explains how healthy lungs work, what SP‑C deficiency is, and how it is managed.
